# Data Protection and Safeguarding Checklist

Before any real data collection:
- Obtain approval from the responsible hospital/diocesan leadership and relevant authorities where required.
- Explain collection in age-appropriate language and obtain consent/assent as applicable.
- Collect only information necessary for the approved purpose.
- Do not place names, phone numbers, addresses, patient numbers, identifiable health details, or case notes in this dashboard.
- Use aggregate counts and assess re-identification risks before sharing.
- Restrict access to authorised staff; secure devices and files.
- Define retention and secure deletion rules.
- Establish safeguarding, confidential referral, and incident-response procedures.
- Have qualified health professionals review education content and referral pathways.
- Do not use AI to diagnose or replace professional judgement.

This checklist is a starting point, not legal advice or a substitute for an organisational data-protection assessment.
