# Proposed Pilot Implementation Plan

## 1. Preparation
- Confirm scope, target groups, safeguarding lead, and referral partners.
- Consult young people, caregivers, schools, church leaders, and health workers.
- Agree on measurable indicators and reporting responsibilities.
- Complete privacy, safeguarding, and risk reviews.

## 2. Build and review
- Test the dashboard with synthetic data only.
- Ask health and monitoring staff to review calculations and accessibility.
- Train authorised staff in confidentiality, safeguarding, and secure device use.

## 3. Approved small pilot
- Begin only after required approvals are in place.
- Record aggregate activity: sessions, participants reached, counselling sessions, and referrals.
- Avoid identifiable data in the dashboard.
- Review data quality and safety regularly.

## 4. Evaluate and improve
- Compare activity with agreed baseline and targets.
- Gather voluntary feedback safely and confidentially.
- Document lessons, limitations, service gaps, and improvements.
- Scale only after review and leadership approval.

## Possible indicators
- Health education sessions delivered.
- Girls reached, reported only in aggregate.
- Counselling sessions and referrals.
- Participant feedback on relevance and accessibility.
- Staff trained and service gaps identified.

No pilot or field-test results are claimed here. Record results only after actual approved implementation.
